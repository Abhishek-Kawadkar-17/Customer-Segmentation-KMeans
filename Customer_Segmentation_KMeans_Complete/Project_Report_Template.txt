PROJECT REPORT: CUSTOMER SEGMENTATION USING K-MEANS

To group customers into segments based on annual income and spending score using the K-Means clustering algorithm.

2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
A business may have customers with different income levels and spending behaviour. Customer segmentation helps summarize these patterns into groups for general marketing analysis.
